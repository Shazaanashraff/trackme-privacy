# Privacy Policy — Pingo (Passenger App) & Pingo Driver

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

This policy covers both Pingo mobile apps published by Shazaan Ashraff:

- **Pingo** (passenger app, package `com.pingo.userapp`)
- **Pingo Driver** (driver app, package `com.pingo.driverapp`)

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights below, contact
**mohamedshazaan7@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### Account information (both apps)
- Name, email address, and password (or Google Sign-In identity, passenger app only)
- Profile photo, if you choose to add one
- Role (passenger or driver) and, for drivers, vehicle/route assignment

### Location data
- **Passenger app:** your device location is used only to show your position on the live map
  while the app is open. It is not collected or stored by our servers.
- **Driver app:** while a driver is on duty, the app collects **precise (GPS) location** and
  transmits it to our servers, **including when the app is in the background**, so that
  passengers and administrators can see the vehicle's real-time position. Location broadcast
  stops when the driver goes off duty.

### Camera
- **Driver app only:** the camera is used to scan a passenger's QR attendance pass when they
  board or alight. Camera images are processed on-device to read the QR code and are **not**
  captured, stored, or transmitted.

### Usage & diagnostic data
- Push notification tokens (to deliver route/service notifications)
- Crash and error diagnostics (passenger app uses Sentry for crash reporting)

### Information we do **not** collect
- We do not access your contacts, photo library (beyond a profile photo you explicitly choose
  to upload), microphone, or SMS.
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 2. How we use this information

- To operate core app features: real-time bus tracking, route browsing, QR-based attendance,
  driver duty management, and notifications.
- To authenticate your account and secure access (including via Google Sign-In, where used).
- To diagnose crashes and improve app reliability.
- We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

---

## 3. How information is shared

- **Location while on duty** is shared with passengers subscribed to that route and with
  administrators, in real time, for the purpose of the tracking service itself.
- **Service providers:** we use third-party infrastructure to operate the app, including:
  - Render for our application server and database
  - Google (Maps SDK, and Sign-In for the passenger app)
  - Sentry, for crash/error reporting (passenger app)
  - Expo/EAS, for app updates and push notifications
- We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties, and we do not share it for third-party
  advertising purposes.
- We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property
  of our users or the public.

---

## 4. Data retention & deletion

- Account and profile data is retained while your account is active.
- Historical vehicle location is **not** retained — the backend stores only a vehicle's current
  position, which is overwritten on each update.
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting
  **mohamedshazaan7@gmail.com**. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30
  days, except where retention is required by law.

---

## 5. Children's privacy

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
information, contact us and we will remove it.

---

## 6. Your choices

- You can disable location permissions in your device settings; the driver app cannot broadcast
  your vehicle's position if location access is denied, and the passenger app cannot show your
  position on the map.
- You can decline camera access; QR scanning (driver app) will not function without it.
- You can delete your account as described in section 4.

---

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
"Last updated" date above.
